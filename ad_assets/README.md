## 🚀 Quickstart for Video Editors
1. **Drag-and-Drop:** Open Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, CapCut, or Remotion, and import the `ad_assets/` folder.
2. **Overlay Logos:** Use files from `01_logos_and_brand/` as alpha-channel overlays over video clips.
3. **Showcase Real Features:** Use screenshots from `02_website_ui_walkthrough/` and ready-made motion cards from `03_button_and_feature_explorations/` to show viewers the real product in action.
4. **Follow the Storyboard:** Follow `04_video_ad_production_pack/COMMERCIAL_SCRIPT_30S_GENERAL_PUBLIC.md` or `COMMERCIAL_SCRIPT_60S_FULL_EXPLAINER.md` for exact timing and voiceover sync.
