## 3. Recommended AI Video Generation Settings
* **Motion Strength:** `3` to `5` (Keep motion smooth and grounded; avoid erratic AI warping).
* **Camera Controls in Runway/Kling:**
  - `Horizontal Pan:` `0.5`
  - `Zoom In:` `0.8` (Gentle, steady drift)
  - `Roll:` `0.0` (Never roll camera on architecture)
* **Aspect Ratio:**
  - For YouTube / Web: `--ar 16:9`
  - For Reels / TikTok: `--ar 9:16`
