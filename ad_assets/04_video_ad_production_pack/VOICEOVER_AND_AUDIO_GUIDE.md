# 🎙️ VOICEOVER, AUDIO & SOUND DESIGN GUIDE
**Acoustic Architecture for the First ScoutIt Advertisement**

---

## 1. Voice Persona & Direction

| Attribute | Guideline | What to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tone** | Conversational, modern, articulate, grounded, trustworthy. | Cheesy TV car salesman, stiff British corporate narrator, robotic AI mono-tone. |
| **Accent / Cadence** | Neutral Filipino-English or clear contemporary international English with natural warmth and authentic phrasing. | Over-dramatized Hollywood trailer voice, hyper-fast radio pitch. |
| **Pacing** | **2.0 to 2.4 words per second**. Deliberate pauses on key punches ("Project NOAH", "Zero sign-up walls", "Direct contact"). | Rushing through sentences without breathing room. |
| **Mic Setup** | Clean broadcast condenser or dynamic mic (Shure SM7B, Rode NT1) with slight proximity effect, dry room, subtle 80Hz high-pass filter. | Reverb-heavy room echo, muffled phone microphone recording. |

---

## 2. Music & Soundtrack Recommendations

* **BPM (Beats Per Minute):** **124 to 128 BPM**.
* **Genre:** Modern Minimal Tech-House / Melodic Electronic / Ambient Trap.
  - Deep 808 sub-bass kicks that hit cleanly on scene transitions.
  - Crisp hi-hats and warm Rhodes or analog synth arpeggios in the background.
  - Minor key with an uplifting, resolved resolution at the finale.
* **Audio Ducking:**
  - Music Level during Voiceover: **-18 dB to -20 dB** (voice remains 100% crisp and intelligible).
  - Music Level during Instrumental Drops / Hook / Outro: **-8 dB to -10 dB**.
