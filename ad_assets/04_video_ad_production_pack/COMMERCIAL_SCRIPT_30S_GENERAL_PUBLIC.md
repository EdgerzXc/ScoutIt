## The Big Idea
Searching for a home, apartment, or commercial space in Metro Manila is notoriously frustrating: fake prices ("PM for price"), distorted photos, streets that flood knee-deep, and brokers who ghost you. ScoutIt brings **Physical Reality** to real estate—clean dark-mode map, real verified photos, Project NOAH flood checks, and direct contact with verified owners.

## Production Notes for the Video Editor
1. **Aspect Ratio Options:**
   - 9:16 Vertical (1080 × 1920) for TikTok / Reels: Keep all text cards centered within the safe 80% box.
   - 16:9 Widescreen (1920 × 1080) for YouTube / Web: Use full resolution screens from `02_website_ui_walkthrough/`.
2. **Color Balance:**
   - Maintain the deep obsidian blacks (`#08080A`) and punchy amber highlights (`#E8AE3C`).
   - Do not apply generic orange/teal film luts that wash out the gold.
3. **Voiceover Vibe:**
   - Warm, articulate, friendly, and relatable (like a tech-savvy friend showing you a life hack).
   - Never sound like an aggressive late-night TV infomercial salesman.
