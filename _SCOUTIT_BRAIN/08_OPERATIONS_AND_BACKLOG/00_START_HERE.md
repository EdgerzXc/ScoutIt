# Start Here — Operations & Backlog

**This is the section router.** For execution, immediately open
[[08_OPERATIONS_AND_BACKLOG/ACTION/00_MASTER_ACTION_PLAN|Master Action Router]].

## What lives here

- **Urgent:** broken current work and honest pre-pilot blockers
- **Active:** approved engineering that can proceed
- **Owner:** decisions, dashboards, counsel, credentials, physical devices
- **Waiting:** blocked work with a named unblock condition
- **Future:** deliberately deferred or trigger-gated work
- **Done:** monthly closure evidence
- **Inbox:** preserved findings and legacy ledgers awaiting reconciliation
- **Rules:** binding implementation and frontend standards

The live queues contain tasks. Specifications describe behavior. Implementation
records and handoffs preserve evidence. None of those other documents can assign
work by themselves.

## Why this structure exists

The earlier master ledger grew to 4,503 lines and mixed completed, contradicted,
future, recurring, owner-gated, and genuinely open work. Agents could find an
unchecked historical box and rebuild an accepted surface. On 2026-08-22 the
ledger was preserved intact in the non-executable Inbox and replaced by bounded
queues.

**The prevention rule:** one item, one ID, one active home. Closing or moving an
item removes it from the previous queue in the same change.
