# Waiting and blocked

> Every item names the exact unblock condition. Waiting is not an invitation to
> improvise around owner approval or external state.

## W-001 — Showcase mobile controls below the 44px touch floor

Ten controls on /showcase remain approximately 32px tall at mobile widths.
The Showcase source is owner-approved and checksum locked.

**Unblocks when:** the owner chooses either (a) enlarge the track and controls to
44px and reviews the changed composition, or (b) accept and document this surface
as an intentional density exception.

## W-002 — True Light Mode pilot decision

Light Mode is not accepted across all public/authenticated states.

**Unblocks when:** the owner chooses full remediation now or consistent pilot
withholding across desktop and mobile.

## W-003 — Production migration application

Prepared migrations and wrappers do not authorize live database changes.

**Unblocks when:** the owner approves the exact reconciled migration, backup,
verification, rollback, and one-at-a-time order.

## W-004 — Real-device acceptance

Automated responsive/accessibility checks do not replace physical-device review.

**Unblocks when:** a stable intended release is deployed and the owner completes
the iPhone/Android, zoom, screen-reader, auth, navigation, and pilot journeys.

## W-005 — Payment-provider completion

Provider-neutral payment logic may be designed, but commercial integration and
activation remain gated.

**Unblocks when:** Philippine entity eligibility, GCash/Maya needs, recurring
billing, refunds, settlement, invoicing, and counsel review produce an owner choice.

## W-006 — Search Console sitemap processing follow-up

Verification and sitemap submission are already complete.

**Unblocks when:** the submitted sitemap status is re-read and either records
Success or produces a reproducible fetch defect.
